Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

Kyra Wellness & Spa respects your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information when you visit our website or contact us about wellness services.

Information we collect

When you contact us by WhatsApp, phone, email or an appointment enquiry, you may provide your name, phone number, preferred service, preferred appointment date or time, and any message you choose to send.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to enquiries, check appointment availability, provide requested service information and communicate about your booking. An enquiry does not automatically confirm an appointment.

Analytics and cookies

We use Google Analytics and Google Tag Manager to understand website usage and improve the website. These services may use cookies or similar technologies. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by the communication and analytics services used to operate our website and respond to your enquiry.

Data security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your choices

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information we hold by contacting us. You may also choose not to provide information; however, this may limit our ability to respond to an appointment enquiry.
